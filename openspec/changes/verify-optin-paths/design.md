# Design: verify-optin-paths

## Method
Scratch lake on the homelab (`~/baseballpy-scratch/home`, Python 3.12, `polars[rtcompat]`, `duckdb==1.4.4`), season 2023. Scratch scripts are never committed; evidence is the `catalog status` output and the audit log.

## Findings
- 2026-10-28, per-game family (`max_games=20`, first 20 final games of 2023 across all sports): 13 of 13 per-game datasets ingested in 127 s (boxscore info/officials/pitching notes/players/teams/top performers, feed plays/play events/linescore innings/officials, linescore, win probability, play-by-play); a re-run of all 13 served every request from cache (6 s). `quarantine list` was empty.
- Real games exposed `schema_drift` the generator's three sample games had not: `reviewDetails.*` (challenge/overturn), `matchup.batterHotColdZoneStats.stats`, `team.parentOrgId`/`parentOrgName` (minor-league games). Root cause: `SAMPLE_GAMES` plus one 2015 game is too narrow. Fix: the generator samples a spread of games (first, middle, last) over four dates and the minor-league levels (`SAMPLE_GAME_DAYS`); all 13 game schemas regenerated (26 sample games, 0 failed requests), the drift fields are typed columns, and the column dictionaries were regenerated.
- 2026-10-28, per-player family (homelab, season 2023, `max_people=25` default families; 5 people for `metrics`; 5 people with opposing team 147 / player 543037 for `matchups`): 132, 136 and 149 endpoints, 45,215 + 8,108 + 8,599 rows, 3,300 + 1,075 + 745 requests, 943 s in all, quarantine empty; re-runs served the repeated people from cache. Endpoints with 0 rows were checked to be empty for the sampled people (for example ZiPS hitting and `metric_*_hitting` for the first alphabetical pitchers, pitching/catching splits for hitters), not failures.
- Real data warned `schema_drift` the generator's ten sample people had not shown: `numLeagues` (year-by-year, playoffs, advanced), `league.*` (last-x-games, date-range advanced hitting), `stat.inheritedRunnersScored` (rankings), `numTeams` (single season). Root cause: no sampled player had played in both leagues. Fix: `SAMPLE_PEOPLE` gains Scherzer, Verlander (both leagues), Cruz (many teams) and Skenes (rookie with minor-league games); all 129 person-scoped schemas regenerated (14 sample people, 0 failed requests except the known provider 404s) and the column dictionaries rebuilt.
- Pre-existing, not a regression: `people_stats_vs_player_hitting_opp_team` and `_pitching_opp_team` report `key_unique_per_request: false` both before and after (their documented key is only `person_id`; ADR policy from `complete-source-coverage`: the documented key is the best available and the description says it is not unique). No row is lost (parts are appended, not upserted by key); finding a unique key would need the split dimensions of those payloads: left open.
