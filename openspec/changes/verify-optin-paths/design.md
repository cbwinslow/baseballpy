# Design: verify-optin-paths

## Method
Scratch lake on the homelab (`~/baseballpy-scratch/home`, Python 3.12, `polars[rtcompat]`, `duckdb==1.4.4`), season 2023. Scratch scripts are never committed; evidence is the `catalog status` output and the audit log.

## Findings
- 2026-10-28, per-game family (`max_games=20`, first 20 final games of 2023 across all sports): 13 of 13 per-game datasets ingested in 127 s (boxscore info/officials/pitching notes/players/teams/top performers, feed plays/play events/linescore innings/officials, linescore, win probability, play-by-play); a re-run of all 13 served every request from cache (6 s). `quarantine list` was empty.
- Real games exposed `schema_drift` the generator's three sample games had not: `reviewDetails.*` (challenge/overturn), `matchup.batterHotColdZoneStats.stats`, `team.parentOrgId`/`parentOrgName` (minor-league games). Root cause: `SAMPLE_GAMES` plus one 2015 game is too narrow. Fix: the generator samples a spread of games (first, middle, last) over four dates and the minor-league levels (`SAMPLE_GAME_DAYS`); all 13 game schemas regenerated (26 sample games, 0 failed requests), the drift fields are typed columns, and the column dictionaries were regenerated.
- 2026-10-28, per-player family (homelab, season 2023, `max_people=25` default families; 5 people for `metrics`; 5 people with opposing team 147 / player 543037 for `matchups`): 132, 136 and 149 endpoints, 45,215 + 8,108 + 8,599 rows, 3,300 + 1,075 + 745 requests, 943 s in all, quarantine empty; re-runs served the repeated people from cache. Endpoints with 0 rows were checked to be empty for the sampled people (for example ZiPS hitting and `metric_*_hitting` for the first alphabetical pitchers, pitching/catching splits for hitters), not failures.
- Real data warned `schema_drift` the generator's ten sample people had not shown: `numLeagues` (year-by-year, playoffs, advanced), `league.*` (last-x-games, date-range advanced hitting), `stat.inheritedRunnersScored` (rankings), `numTeams` (single season). Root cause: no sampled player had played in both leagues. Fix: `SAMPLE_PEOPLE` gains Scherzer, Verlander (both leagues), Cruz (many teams) and Skenes (rookie with minor-league games); all 129 person-scoped schemas regenerated (14 sample people, 0 failed requests except the known provider 404s) and the column dictionaries rebuilt.
- Pre-existing, not a regression: `people_stats_vs_player_hitting_opp_team` and `_pitching_opp_team` report `key_unique_per_request: false` both before and after (their documented key is only `person_id`; ADR policy from `complete-source-coverage`: the documented key is the best available and the description says it is not unique). No row is lost (parts are appended, not upserted by key); finding a unique key would need the split dimensions of those payloads: left open.
- 2026-10-28, all-time pools (homelab, in progress when written): the first eight career pools ingested (135,539 hitting, 122,603 pitching, 345,384 fielding, 16,970 catching rows; advanced variants 135,539, 128,441, 345,384 and 16,970), 20 requests each, 13 s to 17 min per pool. `stats_single_season_pitching` followed with 52,880 rows in 29 min.
- `stats_stats_single_season_hitting` failed with `ReadTimeout` after 17 min. Root cause: a deep offset page of the pool takes longer than the 30 s request timeout, and every retry hit the same limit. Fix: `HttpClient.get`/`get_sync` take a per-call `timeout`, and all-time pools page with `HEAVY_PAGE_TIMEOUT` (180 s); test `test_a_per_call_timeout_overrides_the_configured_one`. The default timeout for every other request is unchanged.
- The two career fielding pools warned `schema_drift` for catcher-only fields (`catcherERA`, `catchersInterference`, `caughtStealing`, `caughtStealingPercentage`, `pickoffs`, ...): the generator sampled only the first 50 rows, none of them a catcher. Fix: the generator also samples pages at offsets 500, 5,000 and 40,000 of every paginated endpoint; both schemas regenerated with those columns typed.

## Historical window (2015-2024, homelab, `ingest statcast retrosheet lahman chadwick --seasons 2015-2024 --concurrency 1`)
- 2026-10-28: 63 jobs, all `completed`, 2.62 h of job time, 7.5 GB lake, empty quarantine, `catalog audit` reported no findings. Peak RSS of a re-run was 1.2 GB.
- Resume: the identical command re-run finished in 46 s (35 of 63 jobs `skipped`, the rest re-fetched the small leaderboard/reference jobs) and the lake size did not change.
- Totals against known figures: Retrosheet `gamelogs` per season 2,429 / 2,428 / 2,430 / 2,431 / 2,429 / 898 / 2,429 / 2,430 / 2,430 / 2,429 (2015-2024); the MLB schedule has 2,430 per full season and 900 for 2020, so the gaps are games not played (2020: two cancelled), and 2018's 2,431 includes the tie-breaker. Statcast regular-plus-post pitches per season 795k-835k (336,648 in the 60-game 2020), and `pitches_minors` 175k (2021, partial) to 889k.
- Statcast 2023 holds 834,954 pitches over 2,955 distinct `game_pk`, more than the 2,430 regular-season games: the season window also covers postseason and exhibition games Savant serves; not a duplicate (the grid windows never overlap).
- 2026-10-28, Statcast game feed (homelab, season 2023, `--datasets game_feed --limit 20`): 20 game feeds fetched in 25 s into `raw/statcast/game_feed` (21 files with the manifest, 50 MB); the re-run was served from the lake with no warnings and no quarantine. The feeds are kept as raw bytes (no processed dataset), which is the intent of the registry entry.
- 2026-10-28, on-demand datasets (homelab, `MlbApiSource.endpoint`): `people_changes` (`updated_since=2026-10-20`) 128 rows x 52 columns in 0.7 s, `game_changes` (`2026-10-27`) 21 rows x 70 columns, `people_search` for `Judge` (5 rows) and `Ohtani` (6 rows), no errors. `people_search` warned `schema_drift` for `pronunciation`: Judge's record lacks it, so the one-name sample missed it (Smith, Garcia and Ohtani carry it). Fix: the generator samples four search names (`SAMPLE_SEARCH_NAMES`); the schema is regenerated with the typed column.

## All-time pools, complete run (homelab, 2026-10-29, after ADR 40)
- 57 pool endpoints ran (`optin5.out`): 42 INGESTED, 2,265,447 rows in all; 15 failed with a root cause each:
  - 3 `stats_stats_single_season_{hitting,fielding,advanced_fielding}`: HTTP 503 after 170-262 s. The 180 s page timeout works (the earlier `ReadTimeout` is gone); the provider itself gives up on deep offsets of the 190k-row pool. Not a client defect; the pitching pool (52,880 rows) ingests, so the hitting-style pools need smaller pages or a per-season split (open, see the backlog).
  - 12 `stats_stat_splits_*` and `stats_career_stat_splits_{pitching,fielding}`: HTTP 406 (`Not Acceptable`). Correction (2026-10-29, same day): this is NOT specific to `statSplits`. A control through the project client and from the homelab gave 406 for every league-wide `v1/stats` request (`stats=season`, with or without `playerPool`, `limit`, `sitCodes`, a browser User-Agent), while `v1/teams` and `v1/people/592450/stats` answer 200 from both hosts. The route answered 200 earlier that day (the career and single-season pools ingested), so the 406s began mid-run and persist: a provider-side change or block on `v1/stats`, not an unsupported combination. They are NOT marked unsupported; B-30 tracks it (retest later; a full `gen_mlb_schemas.py` run during the block wrote an empty `PLAYER_STAT_COMBOS` because phase 1 saw only non-200 answers).
- `schema_drift`: `team_stats_year_by_year_{hitting,pitching}` advanced variants carry fields the sample missed (`stat.ballsInPlay`, `stat.flyHits`, `stat.groundOuts`, ...); they are regenerated with the real-run schema regeneration (B-03).
