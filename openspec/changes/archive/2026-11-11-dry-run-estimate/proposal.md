# Proposal: `ingest --dry-run` says what you are about to download

## Why

The owner wants other people to use baseballpy easily and to know what they are downloading. Today
`baseballpy ingest ... --dry-run` prints only Source, Task, Params and Status: a user cannot tell whether a
command is 20 MB or 30 GB, or whether they already hold it. `catalog status` shows what is held and why
something is missing, but nothing answers "what will this command fetch?" before it runs.

## What

- **A measured table of typical sizes**, generated by a script from a real lake
  (`scripts/measure_dataset_sizes.py` -> `src/baseballpy/core/typical_sizes.json`): per registered dataset, the
  median bytes of one season folder (season-split datasets) or the total bytes (others), and how many seasons were
  measured. Measured values only; a dataset never measured is absent, not guessed.
- **`baseballpy/ingest/estimate.py`**: for a selection (sources, datasets, seasons), expand to the registered
  datasets the default selection would fill, count which of those season folders the lake already holds (from the
  Parquet files), and sum the typical sizes of the missing ones. Datasets without a measurement are counted as
  "unknown", not zero.
- **`--dry-run` footer** (and a `summary` object in `--json`): datasets selected, season folders already held,
  season folders to download, "about X GB (typical, measured on <date>)", unknown count.
  No time is promised (the registry has no source-to-host mapping and requests per dataset are not reliable).
- Nothing is downloaded and no network is needed (seasons must be given; with `-s all` the footer says the
  seasons are not estimated).

## Out of scope

A time estimate (requests per dataset are not reliable yet: per-game datasets share URLs); changing what
ingest downloads; the `catalog` commands.
